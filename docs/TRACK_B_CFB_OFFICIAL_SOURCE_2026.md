# CFB ISD official finder and approved zoning reference (Track B)

2026-11-09 source review, no manufactured or machine-readable school-zone GIS. This source-only rollout is independent of CFB Draft PR #149/#154/#155 and any 2026–27 residential certification.

## Public official publisher facts
The district states the School Search system reflects the attendance zones approved by its Board on March 6, 2025, and advertises 2026–27 enrollment. This official site is the authoritative live *address lookup*:
https://www.cfbisd.edu/students/school-maps-locator-system

The published approved 2025–26 rezoning page includes separate elementary, middle and high school changes, including address-sensitive apartment complexes, subdivisions, and feeder impacts:
https://www.cfbisd.edu/departments/facility-support-services/facility-planning-advisory-group/2025-2026-rezoning

It is wrong to infer automatic 2026–27 school certification from a ZIP, house point, 2025 image alone, or a street marked as a school traffic safety zone. Some actual published apartment exceptions exist, but **not** every apartment requires unit-level verification.

## Integrated UI
A permanently accessible, collapsible source section beneath the existing school boundary heading links the official School Search and the district's approved E/M/H rezoning map page. It warns about the **2025–26 historical approval year**, the current Finder for house-specific assignment, and that this dashboard does **not** hold verified CFB school-specific GIS polygons. No school assignment is generated. Both links remain district-hosted, no copied map images/GeoJSON, no network permissions added.
